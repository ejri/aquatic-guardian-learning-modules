# Lesson photographs

These frames are used in the `#/learn` camera demos and in the Colab notebooks.
They are stills from live underwater cameras already listed on Aquatic Guardian’s
Live ocean page (explore.org and partner operators). They are teaching frames,
not a dataset: do not train a released model on them.
